# Surface brief: Flowfokus web apps (portal + site)

Scope: `apps/portal` (staff portal, Operate mode) and `apps/site` (gym site, Persuade mode for public pages). One visual world for both.

Audience and job: front-desk staff and owners complete member, sale, payment, fulfillment, and check-in tasks while a customer waits. Public visitors read a gym's locations and offers.

Constraints: real API data only; no invented metrics, photos, or claims. Local demo is labelled. Sign-in unavailable states are explicit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The interface is the gym's access-control panel. Every record has a lamp state: granted, denied, balance due, delivered. It refuses the generic SaaS dashboard of cards and charts.

OWN-WORLD: Anodized-aluminum light gray ground, white instrument plates with 1px machined edges, graphite text. Lamp colors only for state: signal green (granted, paid), signal red (denied, error), amber (balance due, pending), slate blue for primary actions. Barlow Semi Condensed headings like stamped panel legends; system sans body; tabular numerals everywhere money and time appear.

STORY: Staff see who is in front of them, what they own, and what is owed; they act in one form; the panel answers with a lit result and its reason.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Portal desk: left rail with organization plate and task list; main area leads with the check-in panel (member, location, Check in) and a lamp result plate, then actionable counts (balance due, awaiting fulfillment) as rows linking to work. Site: gym name plate, locations, and offers as a fixed price board.

FORM: Access-control turnstile panel, candidate 5 of 7, seed key a14505d2. Raise from split-flap concourse (declined): fixed-cell tabular alignment for money and times.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
